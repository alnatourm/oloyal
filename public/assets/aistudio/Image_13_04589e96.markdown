## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene, ultra-modern luxury environment inspired by futuristic architectural automation and high-end physical interfaces. The aesthetic bridges digital fluidity with the tactile weight of precision-molded porcelain and warm internal illumination.

### Personality & Tone
- **Sculpted & Tactile:** Elements appear seamlessly molded from a single continuous substrate rather than layered onto flat canvas planes.
- **Architectural & Calm:** Soft, low-contrast gradients evoke warm interior daylight bouncing off matte white ceramic, eliminating digital harshness.
- **Precision Engineered:** High-contrast warm orange gauges cut through ethereal porcelain surfaces, delivering surgical clarity and immediate system feedback.

### Visual Style
A refined fusion of soft neumorphism and modern industrial design. Surfaces rely on dual-axis lighting (top-left light sources casting soft ambient glows counterbalanced by gentle bottom-right umbra shadows) to establish extruded convex volumes and debossed concave receptacles. Micro-interactions reward interaction with realistic mechanical depression and responsive inner lighting.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by pristine ceramic neutrals paired with radiant warm orange illumination.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#FF5722`):** Reserved strictly for active dial tracks, focal switches, energized gauges, and stateful highlights. It serves as visual heat against cool porcelain.
- **Secondary (`#FF7043`):** Utilized for secondary dial arcs, gradient transitions, warm glowing backdrops, and active hover states.
- **Porcelain Canvas (`#F0F2F5`):** The master substrate color from which all controls and embossed elements emerge.
- **Shadow Dark Tint (`#D1D9E6`):** The ambient shadow shade responsible for external drop shadows and inner recess bevels.
- **Highlight White (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure light reflection used on top-left edges to create three-dimensional curvature.
- **Text & Glyphs:** High-contrast charcoal (`#1E2229`) for numerical values, balanced with muted slate (`#788292`) for descriptive metadata and sub-labels.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes structural clarity and mechanical precision through Inter.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Numerical Dominance:** Large metrics (e.g., temperatures, track counters) use light weight (`300`) at expansive scales (`display-xl`) with negative tracking, creating a clinical, high-end appliance readout.
- **Section Headers:** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) in `label-md` or `label-sm` to denote quiet technical control.
- **Body & Data Readouts:** Medium and regular weights ensure legible telemetry under soft lighting conditions without overpowering tactile surface geometry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics precision instrument dashboards with radial and orbital focal centers rather than rigid vertical text columns.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid asymmetric cockpit layout with a primary focal dial anchored centrally or in the primary third, flanked by modular secondary panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Two-column split-panel layout maintaining tactile visual breathing room.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked configuration where rotary controls scale to a compact horizontal dimension without losing finger-accessible touch targets (minimum 48px).

### Spacing Philosophy
Ample outer margins (`margin`: `2.5rem`) and structural element separation (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) are mandatory to let soft convex/concave shadows feather naturally without overlapping artifacts.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is engineered through light simulation rather than high-contrast structural borders or flat color blocking. The simulated light originates from the top-left at a 135-degree angle.

### Tactile States
1. **Convex Extrusion (Floating Buttons, Cards):**
   - Shadow Pair: Top-left `-8px -8px 16px #FFFFFF`, Bottom-right `8px 8px 16px #D1D9E6`.
   - Result: Surface gently rises outward toward the user.

2. **Concave Deboss (Input Wells, Recessed Tracks, Inactive Slots):**
   - Inner Shadow Pair: Top-left `inset 6px 6px 12px #D1D9E6`, Bottom-right `inset -6px -6px 12px #FFFFFF`.
   - Result: Surface is carved inward into the ceramic baseplate.

3. **Active Illumination Glow:**
   - Active state elements add a warm orange aura (`0 0 20px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.35)`), simulating embedded LED luminescence through translucent resin.

## Shapes

Shapes adhere strictly to organic pill capsules, circular dials, and generous soft rectangular radiuses that evoke vacuum-molded ceramic casings.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Primary Radiuses:** Major control modules utilize smooth, large-radius rounding (`rounded-lg` at `2rem`, `rounded-xl` at `3rem`).
- **Interactive Controls:** All standalone buttons, state chips, and status switches are fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` or level `3`).
- **Circular Dials:** Rotary dials, gauges, and radial controllers are locked to a strict 1:1 circular aspect ratio with circular inner rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Convex Pill Button:** Sculpted ceramic button. Default state has dual exterior shadows (`-4px -4px 10px #FFFFFF`, `4px 4px 10px #D1D9E6`). 
- **Pressed State:** Transitions instantly into a debossed inner shadow (`inset 3px 3px 6px #D1D9E6`, `inset -3px -3px 6px #FFFFFF`).
- **Action Highlight Variant:** Filled with `#FF5722`, white iconography, and a soft orange outer glow.

### Radial Track Gauges & Rotary Dials
- Concentric multi-tier circular wells. Outer rim features debossed circular tracks with `#FF5722` arc fills indicating current magnitude.
- Center knob features a convex extruded wheel with a central notch indicator and real-time numeric readouts rendered in `display-xl`.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- Contained within a debossed pill track. The active selection pops upward into a convex white pill with a glowing `#FF5722` icon or label.

### Lists & Grouped Sensors
- Rather than dividing rules, items are housed within shallow indented grooves or sit as individual extruded cards separated by `space-sm`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed deep wells with dual inner shadows. Text input rests within the recess in neutral charcoal, accompanied by glowing orange focus indicators along the inner border.

### Cards & Panels
- Expansive porcelain plates featuring large curved corners (`rounded-xl`), soft dual shadows, and internal concave regions that group sub-controls intuitively.
