Privacy Policy - PassLoyal OS

Last updated: November 11, 2026
Company: PassLoyal OS
Contact: alnatour.m@gmail.com

1. What we collect

When you enroll in a merchant loyalty program through PassLoyal, we collect: your name, phone number, email address, member ID, points balance, and pass type (loyalty, gift card, event ticket, or offer).

2. Why we collect it

To create and manage your loyalty / gift / ticket / offer pass, to show your points balance, and to let you save the pass to Google Wallet or Apple Wallet.

3. What we share with Google Wallet

When you tap "Save to Wallet", we share only the pass details needed to create your pass with Google Wallet: member name, member ID, points / balance, and the merchant's name, logo and brand color. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your pass is active. If you delete your pass or ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep transaction records longer.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your data. Email alnatour.m@gmail.com with your member ID and phone number. You can also remove the pass at any time from Google Wallet - removing it from Wallet does not delete your merchant account, email us if you want full deletion.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, signed requests, and access controls to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

7. Children

PassLoyal is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
